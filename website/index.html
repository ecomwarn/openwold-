FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does OpenWolf change how I use Claude Code?

No. OpenWolf runs entirely through hook scripts that fire automatically. You keep your exact workflow — it just gets cheaper and smarter.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — file index, learning memory, token ledger — stays on your machine. Nothing is sent to external servers.

How much will I actually save?

Across 20 projects and 132+ sessions we measured an average token reduction of 65.8%, with some projects saving 80%. Results vary by project size and usage.

Is it open source?

Yes — the core is licensed under AGPL-3.0 and available on GitHub. Paid plans add team sync, analytics, and support.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month (or yearly with a discount) and you can cancel from your dashboard with one click.
